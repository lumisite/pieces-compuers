Enugu, NG
Who We Are

Every service, one job docket.

Pieces Computers & Engineering Services started as a small computer sales-and-repair counter on Nike Road and grew alongside the businesses, schools, churches and individuals who kept walking in with new requests — a WAEC registration one week, a branded jersey order the next, a faulty inverter the week after. Rather than turn work away, we built out the capacity to handle it all: an IT desk, a print & design bench, a branding press, a registration/documentation counter, and a field crew for electrical and construction jobs.

Today, one visit to our office covers computer sales & repair, design & print, apparel branding, exam and government registrations, and on-site electrical or solar installation — logged and tracked like a proper job docket, from intake to completion.
